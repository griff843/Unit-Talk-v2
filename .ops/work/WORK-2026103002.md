# WORK-2026103002 — Push-triggered closeout waits for merge-SHA CI

Tier: T1
Lane type: governance
Executor: claude

Source: PM directive (2026-10-30), "Keep one meaningful operating-model improvement lane active", measured by closeouts that need a manual replay. Mechanical tier floor is T1 (`.github/workflows/**` is a tier-c prefix); the tier is not lowered.

## Objective

`post-merge-lane-close.yml` starts on the merge push. `ci.yml` on the same merge SHA takes 13 to 17 minutes. A lane that deferred its T1 live-DB precondition to CI therefore always fails G6 on its push closeout ("the staging receipt is not green at merge SHA ... Missing or failing: verify, Writable DB proof (staging only)"). It then needs a manual `workflow_dispatch` replay once CI is green. The last three such lanes all followed this sequence:

| Merge | Push closeout (G6 FAIL) | ci.yml on merge SHA | Replay |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cd350ef8b` (#1802) | 40987061159, 01:38 | 01:38 to 01:52, success | 40988392742 success |
| `b4ab31a3a` (#1801) | 40994374430, 02:54 | 02:54 to 03:11, success | 40995965707 success |
| `104185674` (#1804) | 41004630969, 04:45 | 04:45 to (running) | pending |

## Acceptance Criteria

- On `push`, a new `wait-for-merge-ci` job polls `ci.yml` runs for the merge SHA. `lane-close` starts after the job:
  - The job finishes once every such run has completed, whatever its conclusion. It decides nothing, and every closeout gate still runs unchanged in `lane-close`.
  - It never blocks a closeout indefinitely. The job proceeds with a warning if no run appears within 5 minutes, if the API is unreadable until the deadline, or once 50 minutes have elapsed.
- The wait job holds no concurrency group. The `merge-closeout-mutex` moves from the workflow to the `lane-close` job, so waiting never occupies the mutex or its single pending slot.
- `workflow_dispatch` replays skip the wait and still run `lane-close` (`!cancelled()`), and bot pushes still skip both jobs.
- `scripts/ops/workflow-hardening.test.ts` executes the wait step's script, as written, against a stubbed `gh`. Each of these mutations fails it:
  - completion judged by any run instead of all runs;
  - no absent grace;
  - unreadable treated as completed;
  - `needs` removed;
  - the mutex put on the wait job;
  - `!cancelled()` removed.
- Unchanged: every closeout gate (G6 included), Merge Gate, the required checks, lane admission and `ci.yml`.
