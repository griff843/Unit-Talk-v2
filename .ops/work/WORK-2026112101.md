## Problem

`buildPickPresentation` (shared by `pick-posted` and `official-pick` embeds) appends the line to the selection as ` @ +27.5`. In Discord, `@` reads as odds, and the selection usually already states the line, so a member sees "LeBron James Over 27.5 @ +27.5".

## Objective

The pick title shows the line once and never in odds form.

## Acceptance Criteria

- When the selection already states the line's number (compared numerically, so "27.50" matches 27.5), nothing is appended.
- When the selection does not state the line, append it with no `@`.
  - Spreads, handicaps, run lines and puck lines (including `run_line` and `puck_line` keys) keep their sign.
  - Totals and props show the bare number.
- With no line, the title is the selection alone.
- Tests in `packages/domain/src/discord-presentation.test.ts` fail on `main` and pass on head.
- Existing consumers still pass: `apps/worker/src/official-picks-embed.test.ts`, `apps/worker/src/delivery-adapters.test.ts` and `apps/discord-bot/src/discord-design-system.test.ts`.
- No change to delivery targets, kill switches, outbox, entitlement, Track Only gating, schema or artwork behaviour.
