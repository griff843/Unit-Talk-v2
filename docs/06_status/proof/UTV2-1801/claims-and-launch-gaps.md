## Current-site audit
Old homepage used a typed wordmark, trading-style grid and decorative live-desk labels and animations without public runtime-backed data. Pricing omitted Trial and approved prices, advertised Syndicate, and used blanket feature check marks. Results lacked complete-history context, no capper surface existed, and purchase-style CTAs led to information/support rather than verified access. FAQ/privacy implied cancellation and automatic channel unlock. The canonical URL defaulted to a placeholder, with no approved favicon or OG image.

## Launch gaps
- Commerce: no verified public checkout; no purchase CTAs, new billing logic or commerce activation.
- Results: no public canonical results feed; no invented statistics, mock records or hidden losses.
- Cappers: no publishable public directory/records; truthful fallback, no invented people.
- Discord: no verified public invite or automatic provisioning; illustrative structure is clearly labeled.
- VIP+: no proved feature activation or published price; each capability unavailable/unconfirmed.
- Black Label: future, closed to enrollment.
- Legal: existing Terms/Privacy remain visibly drafts pending counsel. No new legal mechanics were authored.
- Public support: no approved email or invite in site configuration.
- Brand: no conflict; canonical identity used unchanged.
