# Unit Talk Canonical Logo Assets

## Primary digital mark — owner supplied

As of 2026-10-11, Griff's supplied circular **UT / UNIT TALK** mark is the
primary digital logo for compact and identity-first placements.

Canonical source:

- [Primary circular mark](assets/unit-talk-primary-circle.svg)

This asset is a faithful embedded copy of the owner-supplied logo image. It is
stored rather than redrawn so implementation work cannot reinterpret the UT
geometry, reconnect strokes, alter the T line, substitute typed lettering, or
silently fall back to a different monogram.

Use the primary circular mark for:

- public website header/navigation
- mobile navigation
- social profile/avatar placements
- Discord server/icon placements
- compact badges and small identity surfaces

The supplied circle includes the `UNIT TALK` text inside the mark. Do not add a
second typed `UNIT TALK` label beside it in compact navigation.

## Reuse rules

Do not redraw, regenerate, trace, reinterpret, or replace either approved logo
geometry with typed lettering.

For compact digital identity, use `unit-talk-primary-circle.svg`.

Use the secondary horizontal/full lockups only where the composition genuinely
benefits from a separate wordmark and there is enough space to preserve its
geometry.

Preserve aspect ratio and internal proportions. Supporting taglines, gold
treatments, glows, photography, or other visual treatments must not modify the
logo geometry itself.

The older `unit-talk-original-logo-reference.png` and
`unit-talk-flat-logo-concepts.png` remain historical references only.
