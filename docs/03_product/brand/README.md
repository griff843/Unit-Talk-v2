# Unit Talk Canonical Logo Assets

The current canonical Unit Talk identity is the **Concept 06 soft-geometry UT
monogram** with the **Custom Geometric C wordmark**, finalized on 2026-10-02.
The black and white SVG masters and PNG/icon exports are in [assets/](assets/),
imported unchanged from the prepared `unit-talk-production-assets.zip` package.

## SVG Masters

- [Black monogram](assets/unit-talk-mark.svg) and [white monogram](assets/unit-talk-mark-white.svg).
- [Black wordmark](assets/unit-talk-wordmark.svg) and [white wordmark](assets/unit-talk-wordmark-white.svg).
- [Black horizontal lockup](assets/unit-talk-horizontal.svg) and [white horizontal lockup](assets/unit-talk-horizontal-white.svg).
- [Black stacked lockup](assets/unit-talk-stacked.svg) and [white stacked lockup](assets/unit-talk-stacked-white.svg).
- [Dark app icon](assets/unit-talk-icon-dark.svg) and [Discord icon](assets/unit-talk-discord.svg).

The wordmark uses vector outlines and has no runtime font dependency. Its open
geometric A is intentional. [brand-assets.json](assets/brand-assets.json) records
the canonical master filenames and identity version.

## Reuse Rules

Future product and marketing work must reuse the **exact master geometry**.
Do not redraw, regenerate, reinterpret, or replace the monogram or wordmark with
typed lettering. Use the SVG masters directly, or embed their exact paths when
static SVG assets are unsuitable.

Preserve aspect ratio and internal proportions. Use white artwork on dark
backgrounds and black artwork on light backgrounds. Prefer the horizontal lockup
where space permits and the monogram alone for compact navigation, icons, and
avatars. Keep clear space approximately equal to the monogram's vertical stroke
width. Supporting taglines and visual treatments must not alter master geometry
or become part of the canonical artwork.

The older `unit-talk-original-logo-reference.png` and
`unit-talk-flat-logo-concepts.png` are historical references, superseded by these
canonical assets.
